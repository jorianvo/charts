Messages from the EMR

The EMR sends each new result as an HL7 v2 message (ORU^R01). The connector stores it as a document and registers it with its bridge.

    Patient record

    Pick a patient to see their documents from every hospital in the network, not just this one.

      How it works

      1. The EMR sends a result. An HL7 v2 message arrives at the connector, inside the hospital.
      2. Register document ITI-42 The connector tells its bridge in the cloud that the document exists, over the hospital's site-to-site VPN.
      3. Find documents ITI-18 A clinician picks a patient; the connector asks its bridge what exists for that patient.
      4. Ask other hospitals ITI-38 The bridge asks the bridges of the other hospitals, through the central bridge.
      5. Fetch from other hospital ITI-39 Opening another hospital's document fetches it the same way. Own documents come straight from the bridge ITI-43.
